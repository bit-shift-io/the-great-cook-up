# 5-Ingredient Chicken Pie with Parmentier Potato Topping

Serves: 4
Time: 5 min prep + 40 min cook

## Ingredients

* 500g pkt Coles Finest Chef Inspired Parmentier Potatoes with Herb Butter plus Garlic
* 1 Tbl extra virgin olive oil
* Coles Frozen Carrot, Corn & Peas
* 1 Coles RSPCA Approved Whole Chicken Roast, skin and bones discarded, meat roughly shredded
* 480g tub Coles Kitchen Carbonara Sauce
* 120g (1 1/2 cups) pre-grated tasty cheese

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200C/180C fan forced. Line a large baking tray with baking paper. Arrange potatoes on the prepared tray. Roast, tossing halfway, for 20 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook frozen vegetables, stirring occasionally, for 3-4 minutes or until vegetables start to soften. Add chicken, carbonara sauce and 125ml (1/2 cup) water. Season. Stir to combine. Transfer mixture to an 8-cup (2L) baking dish.
3. Evenly spoon potatoes over the chicken mixture. Sprinkle with cheese. Bake for 15-20 minutes or until the potato is golden and crunchy, and the pie is heated through. Serve.

## Notes

If you have a large ovenproof frying pan, add the potatoes directly on top of the filling in the pan and bake, instead of transferring to a baking dish.

Bake long enough that the cheese melts through the potatoes so they become golden and crispy.
