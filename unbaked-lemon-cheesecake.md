# Unbaked Lemon Cheesecake

Serves: 

Time: 

## Ingredients

* 180g ginger nut biscuits, crushed
* 90g melted butter
* 227g cream cheese, at room temperature
* 400g condensed milk
* 1/3 cup lemon juice


## Extras

* Fresh raspberries and blueberries, to decorate
* Icing sugar or chocolate flake, to dust


## Method

1. Mix biscuits and butter together well.
2. Press into foil-lined 23cm pie plate.
3. Chill.
4. Beat cream cheese until softened.
5. Gradually beat in condensed milk and lemon juice.
6. Spoon into pie plate.
7. Chill. Best made 24 hours prior to serving.
8. To serve: remove the cheesecake from the pie plate. Peel off the foil and arrange on a platter.
9. Just before serving decorate the top with the fruit and dust with icing sugar or chocolate flake.
