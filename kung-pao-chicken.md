# Kung Pao Chicken

Serves: 4
Time: 15 min prep + 10 min cook

## Ingredients

* 500g chicken thigh, cut into bite-sized pieces

Sauce:

* 2 tsp cornflour
* 1 Tbl light soy sauce
* 1 1/2 Tbl dark soy sauce
* 2 Tbl Chinese black vinegar
* 1 Tbl Shaoxing wine
* 3 Tbl sugar
* 1/2 tsp sesame oil
* 1/3 cup water

Stir fry:

* 2 Tbl peanut oil
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tsp finely chopped ginger
* 6-10 dried chillies, cut into 2cm pieces, most seeds discarded
* 3 spring onions, cut into 2cm pieces, white and green parts separated
* 1 1/2 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorns
* 3/4 cup whole unsalted roasted peanuts

## Method

1. Mix cornflour with light soy until dissolved, then stir in the remaining sauce ingredients except the water. Toss 1 1/2 Tbl of this sauce through the chicken and marinate 10-20 min. Add the water to the remaining sauce.
2. Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Cook garlic, ginger and chillies about 30 seconds until fragrant.
3. Add chicken and cook until white, then add the white spring onion. Cook until chicken is cooked through, about 2 min.
4. Add sauce and Sichuan pepper. Simmer, stirring, until reduced to a thick syrup.
5. Stir in peanuts and green spring onion. Taste, add more Sichuan pepper if wanted.
6. Serve immediately with rice.

## Notes

Original: https://www.recipetineats.com/kung-pao-chicken/

Substitutes: black vinegar -> 1.5 Tbl rice wine vinegar; Shaoxing -> dry sherry or sake. Dried chilli heat varies, taste one first.
