# Chocolate Mousse in Minutes

Serves: 6
Time: 20 min prep + 1 hour chill

## Ingredients

* 300g Cadbury baking dark chocolate (45% cocoa solids), roughly chopped
* 3 eggs, at room temperature
* 1/4 cup (55g) caster sugar
* 1 Tbl good-quality cocoa powder, sifted
* 300ml thickened cream, chilled
* Whipped cream, to serve
* Grated chocolate, to serve

## Method

1. Place the chocolate in a heatproof bowl over a pan of gently simmering water (don't let the bowl touch the water). Stir until melted. Remove bowl from heat and set aside to cool slightly.
2. Place eggs and sugar in a large bowl and beat with electric beaters for 5 minutes, or until mixture is pale, thick and doubled in volume.
3. Fold cocoa powder and cooled chocolate into the egg mixture until combined. (The cooled chocolate and egg mixture need to be close to the same temperature to prevent the chocolate from seizing.)
4. In a separate chilled bowl, whip cream until thickened (be careful not to over-beat).
5. Use a large metal spoon to carefully fold the cream into the chocolate mixture, trying to keep the mixture as light as possible.
6. Spoon into 6 serving glasses and chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour (or preferably overnight).
7. Remove from fridge 15 minutes before serving. Top with extra whipped cream and grated chocolate to serve.
