# 17-Minute Creamy Japanese Chicken Curry

Serves: 4
Time: 2 min prep + 15 min cook

## Ingredients

* 1 Tbl peanut oil
* 650g chicken tenderloins
* 1 small red onion, sliced into thin wedges
* 3 x 18g cubes S&B Golden Curry Mild Curry Sauce Mix
* 1 bunch broccolini, trimmed, halved lengthways
* 270ml can coconut cream
* 60g pkt baby spinach
* Lime wedges, to serve
* Steamed medium grain white rice, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a wok or frying pan over high heat. Add the chicken and cook, turning halfway, for 5 minutes or until nearly cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
2. Add half the onion to the wok. Reduce heat to low and stir-fry for 1 minute or until soft. Add 375ml (1 1/2 cups) water and the curry blocks. Cook, stirring, for 1 minute or until the curry dissolves.
3. Add the broccolini and simmer, stirring often, for 2 minutes or until tender-crisp. Use a slotted spoon to transfer to the plate with the chicken.
4. Add the coconut cream to the wok. Simmer for 5 minutes or until the sauce has thickened. Tear the chicken into pieces. Return the chicken and broccolini to the wok with the spinach. Stir for 1 minute to heat through.
5. Serve sprinkled with the remaining onion, with lime wedges and steamed rice.

## Notes

Original: https://www.taste.com.au/recipes/17-minute-creamy-japanese-chicken-curry-recipe/m8untj1q

Substitutes: peanut oil -> any neutral oil or sesame oil; chicken -> tofu, crumbed eggplant or pre-baked crumbed schnitzel; broccolini -> any greens (snow peas, Asian greens); rice -> brown rice or quinoa. A splash of regular soy sauce adds depth.
