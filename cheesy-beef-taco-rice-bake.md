# One-Pan Cheesy Beef Taco Rice Bake

Serves: 4
Time: 10 min prep + 50 min cook

## Ingredients

* 1 Tbl olive oil
* 500g beef mince
* 1 red capsicum, deseeded, coarsely chopped
* 1 red onion, coarsely chopped
* 30g packet taco seasoning
* 2 Tbl tomato paste
* 300g (1 1/2 cups) long-grain white rice
* 400g can black beans, rinsed, drained
* 1 tsp chicken stock powder
* 120g (1 1/2 cups) coarsely grated cheddar
* Sour cream, to serve
* Sliced avocado, to serve

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 200°C/180°C fan forced. Heat the oil in an ovenproof frying pan over medium heat. Cook the beef, breaking up any lumps, for 5 min until browned. Move beef to 1 side of pan. Add the capsicum and onion to the empty side. Cook, stirring, for 5 min until onion mixture softens.
2. Stir beef and onion mixture together. Add taco seasoning and tomato paste. Cook, stirring, for 1 min until aromatic. Add rice and stir until combined. Add beans, stock powder and 750ml (3 cups) water. Bring to the boil.
3. Transfer pan to oven. Bake for 25 min until the liquid has absorbed and the rice is tender. Sprinkle with the cheddar. Bake a further 10 min until the cheddar is melted and golden.
4. Top with sour cream and avocado. Season and serve.

Also works as a filling for taco shells or burritos. Cherry tomatoes are a good fresh addition after cooking. Good for meal prep.
