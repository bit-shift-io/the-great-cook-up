# Tikka Pork & Lentil Curry

Serves: 4
Time: 10 min prep + 20 min cook

## Ingredients

* 1 Tbl olive oil
* 500g pork mince
* 1 red onion, cut into thin wedges
* 60g (1/4 cup) gluten-free tikka masala curry paste
* 4cm piece ginger, peeled, finely grated
* 2 garlic cloves, crushed
* 400g can cherry tomatoes
* 270ml can coconut milk
* 400g can brown lentils, rinsed, drained
* 60g baby spinach

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Cook the pork, breaking up any lumps, for 5 min or until browned. Move pork to 1 side of the pan. Add the onion to the empty side and cook, stirring, 3-4 min until slightly softened.
2. Stir the pork and onion together. Add curry paste, ginger and garlic. Cook, stirring, 1 min until aromatic.
3. Add tomatoes and coconut milk. Cook, stirring, 2-3 min until heated through.
4. Add lentils and spinach. Cook, stirring, 2-3 min until heated through and the spinach is slightly wilted.
5. Serve with rice and naan.

## Notes

Original: https://www.taste.com.au/recipes/tikka-pork-lentil-curry-recipe/joe8p25j
