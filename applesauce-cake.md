# Applesauce Cake

cake serves 1  
muffins serves 6  
prep 5 min  
cook 20 min  

## Ingredients

* 1/2 cup applesauce
* 1/2 cup water
* 3 Tbs oil
* 1 Tbs vinegar
* 1/3 cups sugar
* 3/4 tsp salt
* 1/4 tsp cinamon

* 1/4 tsp allspice (opt)
* 1/2 cup rasisns (opt)
* 1/2 cup shredded coconut (opt)
* 1/2 cup diced walnuts (opt)

* 1 1/2 cups SR flour


## Method

1. preheat oven 180C
2. blend all together except flour
3. gently fold in flour, dont over mix
4. cook 180C ~ 18 mins. Check with skewer.

## Notes

Top with chocolate icing is nice!
